ArchScan Viewer

Open a facial scan export to check it before it goes into CAD. Everything is read in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Mesh — .obj, .ply (ascii or binary), .stl (ascii or binary)
Texture — drop the .jpg alongside the .obj
Landmarks — landmarks.json from the export (adds measurements and the registration markers)
Units — files are read as millimetres, the way ArchScan writes them
