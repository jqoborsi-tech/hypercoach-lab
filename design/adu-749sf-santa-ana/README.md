# Seven Forty-Nine — 749 SF detached ADU
### 3010 S Artesia St, Santa Ana, CA 92704 · APN 412-233-11 · Lot 11, Tract 8280

Design-development set for a two-bedroom, one-bath detached accessory dwelling
unit. Not construction documents; not stamped.

The drawing set is [`drawings.html`](drawings.html) — site plan over the
survey, floor plan, four elevations, a building section with the house drawn

## Issue 03 — fitted to the topographic survey

The survey (Foresight Engineering Inc., 1″ = 10′, 9/04/2026) replaced the
neighbourhood-based lot assumption of Issue 02:

| | Assumed (Issue 02) | Surveyed (Issue 03) |
|---|---|---|
| Lot | 60′ × 120′ | **56.00′ × 106.82′ / 112.94′**, ≈ 6,150 SF |
| Rear yard depth | ~58′ | **33′-4″** (house rear wall to west line) |
| Existing house FF | — | **38.08** |
| Rear yard grade | flat | 37.2 – 38.8, rising to the west |
| Access to rear | side yard | **4′-10″ north side passage**, gated |

A 33-foot rear yard cannot take the 38′ × 21′ bar of Issue 02. The same
749 SF is redrawn as **17′-6″ × 44′-0″ (2.51:1)** along the rear fence.

The depth is arithmetic: 5′-0″ off the rear line keeps the west wall unrated;
10′-10″ to the house lets an assumed property line (CRC R302.1) sit mid-gap
with 5′-5″ to each wall, so neither wall is rated. 33′-4″ − 5′-0″ − 10′-10″ =
17′-6″. The length follows: 56′ less 6′-0″ each side = 44′.

## Plan

A bar this narrow (16′-4″ inside) cannot put two bedrooms side by side, so the
plan changes shape: **a bedroom at each end, the great room between, the entry
recessed into the east face at mid-length.** Bedroom 1 sits behind a 4′ hall
with the bath and laundry. Bedroom 2 opens off the far end of the great room
with its closet on the shared wall as the sound buffer. Kitchen, bath and
laundry line the west (fence) wall. Both bedrooms are 16′-4″ × 8′-6″, each with
a courtyard window and an escape casement in the gable end facing the side yard.

749 remains exact: 17′-6″ × 44′-0″ = 770 SF, less the 4′-0″ × 5′-3″ recessed
entry (21 SF). Gross enclosed 749 SF; interior 651 SF, 99 SF under the
750 SF impact-fee threshold of Gov. Code §66311.5.

Ridge 13′-3″ above grade (16′-0″ limit). 4:12 gable, ridge N–S. Eaves 6″ west
and 12″ east, both with 5/8″ Type X soffits because both fall inside 5′ of a
property line, real or assumed. Rakes 12″, holding 5′-0″. FF 38.70, pad 38.20;
courtyard drains north at 1% to the side passage.

## What the survey settles, and what it raises

Closed: the lot, the house position, the walls, the grades. The 1973 tract is
outside the historic districts; the driveway carries the parking requirement.

Removed by the design: the storage shed at the southwest corner, the concrete
walk to it, a 17-inch and a 12-inch tree inside the footprint. The existing
patio off the house's southwest corner stays and becomes the south end of the
courtyard. Six 8–12 inch trees along the west wall stand ~3′ from the new
footing and will mostly be lost.

Open (see the Redlines sheet): lot coverage at 44.5% (48.8% if the patio is
roofed — the unit is protected by the 800 SF rule in state ADU law either way);
the house's own west eave inside 5′ of the assumed line; the 4′-10″ side passage
as the only path for fire access, utilities and construction; drainage toward
the house; and the usual four — SAMC 41-194 as adopted, transit distance,
sprinklers, the CF1R solar calculation, and a geotechnical report.

Structural, geotechnical, Title 24 energy, and MEP design by others.
