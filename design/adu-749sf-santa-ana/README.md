# Seven Forty-Nine — 749 SF detached ADU, Santa Ana, CA

Design-development set for a two-bedroom, one-bath detached accessory dwelling
unit on a standard Santa Ana R1 lot. Not construction documents; not stamped.

The drawing set lives in [`drawings.html`](drawings.html) — cover, site plan,
floor plan, four elevations, a building section, and three compliance matrices
(zoning, building, energy/green/fees).

## The two decisions the design turns on

**749 SF, measured to the outside face of the exterior walls.** California
prohibits any impact fee on an ADU of 750 SF or less of interior livable space
(Gov. Code §66311.5, formerly §66324, renumbered by SB 543 effective
1 Jan 2026). Drawing to 749 SF *gross* means the unit passes under the
threshold on the strictest reading available to a plan checker; on the interior
measure the city actually applies it is 663 SF, with 86 SF of margin.

The number is achieved exactly: a 28'-0" × 27'-6" rectangle (770 SF) with a
5'-3" × 4'-0" covered entry notched out of the northwest corner (21 SF).
770 − 21 = 749.

**6'-0" setbacks instead of the 4'-0" the city allows.** Taking Santa Ana's
4'-0" concession caps height at 16'-0" and, more expensively, puts two walls
inside the fire-separation distance, where CRC Table R302.1(1) requires
one-hour rated assemblies, caps openings at 25% of wall area, and requires
protected projections. At 6'-0" walls with 12" eaves on those two faces, every
wall and every eave edge holds 5'-0"+ from a property line: no rated
assemblies, no opening limit, ordinary eaves. The lot has the depth to spare.

88.5% efficiency. Circulation is 3.5% of floor area.

Ceilings are 9'-0" flat over the bedrooms and bath; the great room follows the
4:12 rafters to 12'-8" at the ridge. Ridge height 14'-6" above grade against a
16'-0" limit. All-electric: heat-pump mini-split, heat-pump water heater on an
exterior pad, induction cooking, no gas service.

Both sleeping rooms take their emergency escape opening from a **casement**,
not a slider — a 3'×4' horizontal slider clears roughly 5.5 SF of net opening
and fails CRC R310.2.1; the same casement clears about 9.8 SF.

## Site assumptions

Flat interior R1 lot, 50' × 135' (6,750 SF), not in a historic district, flood
zone, or very-high fire hazard severity zone. Existing 1,148 SF single-family
house at a 20' front setback, unsprinklered, with a detached 400 SF garage
mid-lot. Lot coverage with the ADU: 34.0%.

## Verify before a permit set

The city's ADU standards here are taken from published summaries of SAMC
Sec. 41-194 plus state ADU law; santa-ana.org and the Municode text were not
reachable from the session that produced this set. Confirm the adopted
ordinance line by line, along with: the ½-mile transit parking waiver, historic
district status, SAFD's hose-pull measurement (this layout runs ≈129' from the
curb against a 150' limit), sprinkler status of the primary dwelling, the
Title 24 CF1R solar array size, whether a geotechnical report is required
(parts of Santa Ana are in a state-mapped liquefaction zone), and the utility
connection-fee calculation.

Structural, geotechnical, Title 24 energy, and MEP design by others.
