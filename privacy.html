HyperCoach Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

HyperCoach is a free personal strength-training log and planner, available at jqoborsi-tech.github.io/hypercoach-lab. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — your profile (name, age, height, bodyweight, training experience, goal), your training program, workout logs, bodyweight entries and meal-plan preferences — is stored only in your own browser's local storage on your device. The app has no server and no database. The developer cannot see, collect or access your data.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for one permission: access to files that the app itself creates (the drive.file scope). With it, the app:

  • creates and updates a single backup file, hypercoach2-data.json, in your own Google Drive, containing the data listed above;
  • reads that same file back to restore your data on a new device or after reinstalling.

The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. The backup travels directly between your browser and Google; it is never sent to the developer or to any other party. The app does not store your Google password, and the temporary access token Google issues is kept only in your browser's memory while the app is open.

HyperCoach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature described above. It is not sold, not shared, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Sharing

The app does not share your data with anyone. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Third-party services

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the app loads Google's sign-in script when Drive sync is available. Exercise and recipe videos open on YouTube when you tap them. These services may log standard technical information (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: More → Reset all data, or clear the site's data in your browser.
  • In Google Drive: delete hypercoach2-data.json from your Drive.
  • Revoking access: More → Disconnect, or remove HyperCoach at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Health information

HyperCoach provides general fitness and nutrition information, not medical advice. Consult a professional before starting a training or diet program.
